# BRIDGE PURPOSE

The intersection between point guard and shooting guard appears when a player needs to alternate between initiating an advantage and continuing one.

They can organize with the ball.

They can create threat without it.

They can receive an initial advantage created by someone else and quickly turn it into a shot, drive or pass.

They can give up the ball and remain dangerous.

This bridge does not turn both functions into a 50/50 identity.

The primary role pack always keeps the dominant perspective.

Only the nuances that come from moving naturally between organization and perimeter threat are added here.

---

# P03 · YOUR FIRST MAJOR GOAL: BE RELIABLE

**Type:** BRIDGE

In roles that alternate organization and perimeter threat, reliability includes knowing how to change roles without requiring the possession to stop.

Sometimes you will receive to initiate.

Other times you will receive an advantage that has already been created.

And sometimes your best decision will be to pass early, relocate and become an off-ball threat again.

The value lies in recognizing what the possession needs before deciding how much of the ball you need.

---

# P04 · DO NOT TRY TO LEARN EVERYTHING AT ONCE

**Type:** BRIDGE

Do not try to develop every creation tool of a point guard and every scoring solution of a shooting guard at the same time.

First build a reliable connection between both functions:

- receive and decide quickly;
- pass and relocate;
- attack an advantage without stopping it;
- take over handling when needed;
- play without the ball when someone else organizes.

The combination works when you can change function without changing the quality of your decisions.

---

# P06 · LEARN TO PLAY WITH YOUR BODY

**Type:** BRIDGE

This combination requires body control both with the ball and before receiving it.

Changing pace off the dribble is important.

So are decelerating, preparing your feet, changing direction without the ball and arriving balanced to the catch.

The advantage can begin before you touch the ball and continue after you give it up.

---

# P08 · RUN

**Type:** BRIDGE

In transition, decide early what the possession needs.

If you are the best outlet, show for the ball and help organize.

If someone else is already advancing it with an advantage, run wide and turn your position into a threat.

Do not run toward the ball only because you can handle it.

The ability to alternate between handling and spacing makes this combination especially useful.

---

# P09 · UNDERSTAND SCREENS

**Type:** BRIDGE

This combination should recognize two common ways of creating advantage with screens: an on-ball screen with the ball and an off-ball screen before receiving.

The hybrid nuance is being able to move from one read to the other without changing the quality of the decision: provoke a response as the ball handler or prepare an advantageous catch away from the ball.

---

# P10 · TRAIN THE BORING FUNDAMENTALS

**Type:** BRIDGE

The intersection needs to connect two groups of fundamentals:

**handle, pass and read with the ball;**

**prepare your feet, catch and threaten without it.**

Work especially on the transition between the two.

Pass and stay ready.

Catch and decide without needing several dribbles.

Attack a closeout and then find the pass.

Organize an action and immediately become a perimeter option.

The combination loses value if it only works while it has the ball.

---

# P11 · LEARN BASKETBALL OFF THE COURT

**Type:** BRIDGE

When watching this combination, focus especially on changes of role within the same possession.

What happens after the pass? When does creation with the ball return? How is threat maintained while someone else organizes?

Look for smooth transitions between both functions, not two separate repertoires.

---

# P21 · MAKE IT EASY TO PLAY WITH YOU

**Type:** BRIDGE

Being able to organize does not mean you should ask for the ball every time someone else has it.

Being able to score does not mean every catch should end in a shot.

This combination helps the team especially when it can:

- initiate an advantage;
- give up the ball on time;
- maintain spacing;
- receive again without stopping the possession;
- create a second advantage if the defense has already shifted.

Being useful with and without the ball makes it easier to share the court with other creators.

---

# P26 · YOUR DEVELOPMENT PLAN

**Type:** BRIDGE

Across all three phases, pay attention to one specific connection: being able to create with the ball and remain a threat after giving it up.

The combination progresses when both functions support each other without demanding more possession.

---

# P27 · THE STATS THAT DO NOT SHOW UP

**Type:** BRIDGE

In this combination, also pay attention to actions such as:

- initiating an advantage and remaining a threat after passing;
- receiving an advantage created by someone else and preserving it with a quick decision;
- switching between handling and spacing without breaking the rhythm;
- influencing the possession without monopolizing the dribble.

Ask yourself whether you changed functions without the possession losing quality.
